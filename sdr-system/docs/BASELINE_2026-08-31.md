
This does not match the documented V8L1/V10S0 candidate hashes. Addresses in
the historical `0x43c00000` summary region returned bus errors and were absent
from `/proc/iomem`. Therefore the currently loaded FPGA must not be described
as V8L1/V10S0 or assumed to provide summary registers without a fresh identity
and boot-chain audit.
